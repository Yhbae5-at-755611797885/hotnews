Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. What we collect

When the account holder connects TikTok, the Service receives an access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name) from TikTok. No other personal data is collected, and no data from other TikTok users is collected.

2. How it is used

The token is used only to upload the account holder's own videos to their own TikTok account as drafts and to confirm which account is connected.

3. Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer or server. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

4. Deleting data

The account holder can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions). Stored tokens are deleted from the owner's device when access is removed.
